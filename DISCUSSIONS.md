## What this repository's Discussions tab adds

The UniSaaS.UniCORE Discussions space inherits the foundation rules and adds the natural scope of the SaaS-deployment-shape name surface:

- **Naming and classification questions** about the open list of `UniSaaS.UniCORE.<Industry>` classifications (the SaaS-deployment-shape equivalent of `UniCORE.<Industry>`).
- **Deployment-shape questions** about the relationship between `UniCORE.<Industry>` (on-prem deployment shape) and `UniSaaS.UniCORE.<Industry>` (SaaS deployment shape) for the same governed substrate.
- **Cross-references to industry-classified sibling repositories** (`UniSaaS.UniCORE.Law`, `UniSaaS.UniCORE.Healthcare`, etc.) once those are created (per the certification-trigger lock; see `ROADMAP.md`).
- **Practical questions** about how the UniSaaS.UniCORE name relates to specific Vertical Solutions delivered as a SaaS.
