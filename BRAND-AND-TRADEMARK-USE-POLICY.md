## Why this file is short

The canonical `BRAND-AND-TRADEMARK-USE-POLICY.md` for the UniCORE-family programmes lives in the **TrueAI Foundation** repository and is mirrored across the foundation triad (UniVERSE / TrueAI / UniCORE-AI) for consistent reading. The full text — including the badge-denial-on-military structural rule (§4.5) and the two-mechanism structure (§8: CC BY 4.0 governs the work, certification mark governs the badge) — is canonical there.

This file in the **UniSaaS.UniCORE** repository exists to:

1. Make the policy discoverable from the UniSaaS.UniCORE name surface.
2. Confirm that the same rule applies to the `UniSaaS.UniCORE` name and to the open list of `UniSaaS.UniCORE.<Industry>` classified names (the SaaS-deployment-shape sister of the `UniCORE.<Industry>` family).
3. Cross-link to the canonical version.

## Scope extension to UniSaaS.UniCORE

For the avoidance of doubt, the canonical policy applies in full to:

- The `UniSaaS.UniCORE` name itself.
- The open list of `UniSaaS.UniCORE.<Industry>` classified names (`UniSaaS.UniCORE.Law`, `UniSaaS.UniCORE.Healthcare`, `UniSaaS.UniCORE.Banking`, `UniSaaS.UniCORE.Accounting`, `UniSaaS.UniCORE.Space-Industry`, etc., and any future entrants on the list).
- The `Powered by UniCORE AI` and `Built on the TrueAI Foundation` attribution badges, as applied to a SaaS Solution that bears a `UniSaaS.UniCORE.<Industry>` name.

§4.5 (badge-denial-on-military-uses) and §8 (two-mechanism structure: CC BY 4.0 governs the work, certification mark governs the badge) apply identically here. The deployment shape (on-prem vs SaaS) does not change either rule — `UniSaaS.UniCORE.Military` is no more permissible than `UniCORE.Military`, and the certification mark is denied to military uses regardless of deployment topology.

## When industry-classified sibling repos are created

When `UniSaaS.UniCORE.Law`, `UniSaaS.UniCORE.Healthcare`, etc. are created as their own public repositories (see `ROADMAP.md` and the certification-trigger lock), each will carry its own `BRAND-AND-TRADEMARK-USE-POLICY.md` — initially as a cross-reference of this same shape, and graduated to a fuller per-classification text only if and when programme reasons require.
