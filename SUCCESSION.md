## Scope extension to UniSaaS.UniCORE

The UniSaaS.UniCORE name surface and the open list of `UniSaaS.UniCORE.<Industry>` deployment-shape classifications inherit the succession arrangement set out in the canonical document.

Two structural points carry through to this repository:

1. **The gift principle is not subject to succession.** The CC BY 4.0 licence has already been given; succession is about stewardship of the name, the structural rules, and the published documentation, not about the gift surface itself.
2. **The deployment-shape distinction does not change the succession rule.** UniCORE (on-prem deployment shape) and UniSaaS.UniCORE (SaaS deployment shape) are sister name surfaces under the same stewardship; the succession arrangement covers both identically.
